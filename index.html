ABOUT INNOVATEX

Where Engineering Curiosity Meets Real-World Impact

InnovateX 2026 is an experiential festival uniting collegiate innovators, roboticists, software engineers, and digital designers. Conceived as an antidote to routine academic symposiums, InnovateX places participants inside fast-moving, authentic problem spaces.

From 24-hour sprint hackathons with hardware integrations to high-voltage combat robotics and live startup pitches before seasoned venture builders, every challenge is designed to test resilience, technical acumen, and creative audacity.

Autonomous Problem Tracks: Real challenges vetted by industry pioneers.
Mentorship & Sprints: Direct critique and office hours with domain experts.
